# Roadmap: Lottery Pool
Epics in order. An epic never goes into the pipeline as a whole: it is sliced into small tickets first. Only epic 1 is sliced so far.

## Epic 1 · The pool screen
Members, entry countdown and tickets with their lucky numbers, on one screen.
Tickets: 01-members, 02-countdown, 03-tickets, 04-new-numbers

## Epic 2 · Money
Each member's stake (shares × 2.50 € per draw), the pool's total stake, and each member's share of a win, to the cent.
Tickets: not sliced yet

## Epic 3 · Changes to the pool
A member leaves the pool; a weekly stake limit per member.
Tickets: not sliced yet
